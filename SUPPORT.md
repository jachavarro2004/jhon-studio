# Getting help with jhon-studio

jhon-studio is maintained by one person. Pick the place that fits what you
have:

- **A question or an idea to talk through:** open a
  [Discussion](https://github.com/jachavarro2004/jhon-studio/discussions).
- **A bug:** open an
  [issue](https://github.com/jachavarro2004/jhon-studio/issues/new/choose)
  with the bug form. Say what you ran, what you expected and what happened,
  with your `jhon-studio --version` and Node version.
- **A proposal:** open an issue with the proposal form before you write code;
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) describes the rest.
- **A security problem:** never in public; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Before you ask, `jhon-studio doctor` checks node, ffmpeg, Kokoro, the
HyperFrames CLI and Chrome and says how to fix what fails, and every command
has `--help`.
